Delete Your Account & Data

Greenhouse: Mutations · Last updated July 28, 2026

Greenhouse: Mutations only has an account if you've chosen to turn on the optional "Sign in with Google" Cloud Save feature. If you've never signed in, there's no account or cloud data to delete — your progress lives only in local storage on your own device, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Greenhouse: Mutations and go to the Trophies screen.
  2. Scroll to the Cloud Save card.
  3. Tap Delete Cloud Save and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your cloud-stored save data from Firebase Realtime Database. It does not affect your local, on-device save — those are separate. "Sign Out" alone does not delete anything; it just ends your session on this device.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email us at greenhousemutations@gmail.com from the same email address associated with your Google sign-in, with the subject "Delete my Greenhouse: Mutations data." We'll delete your cloud-stored save and confirm by email, typically within a few days.

What gets deleted

  • Your Google account identifier (UID) and the email address associated with it, as far as we retain any link between them and your save.
  • Your saved game progress stored in Firebase Realtime Database under that account.

We don't collect or store anything else tied to your account beyond this. Deletion is permanent — we keep no backup copy.

What isn't affected

Any local save data still on a device you haven't cleared, and any purchase records Google Play itself retains for its own billing/tax/support purposes, are outside our control and unaffected by this request — see Google's own policies for those.
