Pollinate two species together and see what hybrid traits emerge — every plant has its own stem pattern and color genetics.

Research Lab

Run specimens through a three-station research machine to discover new mutated seeds and rare variants.

Herbarium & Sets

Catalog every species and hybrid you've grown, chase rarity tiers, and complete themed collection sets.

Defend your plots from pests and ride out mutation storms — risk and reward baked into every growing season.
